### Designing an Academic Conference Poster

### Objective

The purpose of this assignment is to develop your ability to communicate research concisely and visually to a professional audience in an international conference setting. You will design a conference poster based either on your own doctoral research or on a published paper in your field.

### Task

Design a conference poster that could be presented at an international academic conference in your discipline. You may choose one of the following two options:

Option A: Present your own doctoral research (preliminary or completed).

Option B: Present a critical review of a published journal article of your choice.

The poster must be written in English and must be intelligible to a conference audience that includes both specialists and adjacent-field researchers.

### Design Requirements

Size: A0 portrait or landscape (or the size specified by the target conference). Indicate your chosen dimensions.

Layout: Use a clear visual hierarchy. Sections should be easy to locate. Avoid dense blocks of text.

Typography: Title readable from approximately 1.5–2 meters; body text readable from approximately 1 meter. Use a minimum body font size of 24 pt.

### Presentation Component

In addition to the poster file, prepare a 2-minute poster teaser (advertisement). You will deliver this to the full class, simulating the flash-talk session that precedes a conference poster session. The goal is not to summarize your entire study, but to persuade listeners to come to your poster.
